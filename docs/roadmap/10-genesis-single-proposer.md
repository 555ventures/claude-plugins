## Why this brief

The genesis MoA panel (blind Sonnet proposers → aggregator) is the last surviving committee
after v7 killed the review panel, and the 2026-08-17 research sweep found the same critique
now applies with evidence: capable models outgrow collaboration (Nature MI, Jul 24 2026 —
capability-saturation threshold predicts panel help/hurt, 94% held-out accuracy), panel
verdicts are unreproducible run-to-run (<50% consistent, arXiv 2607.27942, Jul 30 2026) —
disqualifying for one-shot irreversible stack picks — and frontier harnesses have converged
on one-planner-plus-fanned-workers, not symmetric proposer panels. Caveat carried honestly:
no architecture-decision-specific benchmark exists; hence the proof condition below.
