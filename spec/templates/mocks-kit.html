Kit — the shared parts, named once

Every wireframe is instantiated from this page. Name a primitive here or it gets invented once per screen.

page-header

vs the shell header: that one is app-wide, this one is the screen's.

toolbar

vs page-header: a toolbar acts on the collection, a header describes the screen.

list-row

vs table-row: prose-shaped content is a list row, column-shaped is a table row.

table-row

vs list-row: choose this when the columns line up and comparison is the job.

card

vs list-row: a card stands alone on a grid; a row belongs to a sequence.

sheet

a sheet is a task; its compact variant is a decision. Promote the variant to its own primitive only once the product has three real confirm dialogs.

form-field

vs option-group: a field owns one value behind one label.

option-group

the visible-choice count is the boundary: over six, it becomes a field.

blank-state

the only primitive that owns all three states by construction; other primitives instantiate it rather than drawing their own empty case.

pill

this is what absorbs a badge family — name the states here, not once per screen.
