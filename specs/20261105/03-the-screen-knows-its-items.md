# The screen knows its items

## Intent
- You asked: `/spec:status` must be EXACTLY v7's `spec-status.js` — what it prints and how it behaves — on v8's engine: the queue items carry the type and the `after` gate v7's did, migration fills them on niko-hub from the v7 backup, a spec being built in a worktree shows as implementing, non-roadmap specs are always visible, and nothing v8 added to the screen survives.
- I will build:
  - `scripts/status.js` is v7's `spec-status.js` (tag `spec-v7-final`) with its five lib files carried over; only its inputs change: the queue overlay reads v8's v2 file through an adapter, a spec's status reads a live run sidecar in its worktree, the ledger reader returns nothing, and the two `📈` lines and the release clause are deleted. Every other line the screen prints is v7's: v7's `⚡` header, `🚦 solo`, `⏳` branches, `⚠️` decide lines, `🕓`/`⛔`, `🧹 Hygiene` from v7's own anomalies, v7's `✨` lines and footer. No `/spec:fanout` line, no in-flight footer heads, no `⚠️ Pending questions`, no `📥`, no fences, no `🤷` reasons.
  - Queue items gain `type` (prompt items: `plan|prototype|fix|decide`) and `after` (`{brief}` or `{spec}`); `queue add` takes `--type` and `--after`; `queue next`/`list` honour the gate; fanout's PLAN waits an unready spec item; brief items complete with their brief (v7's rule, read from `status.js --json`).
  - `migrate.js` carries `lane`→`type` and `after` from a v1 queue, and on a host already at v2 fills them from the newest git-dir `spec-v7-backup`, printing each fill; idempotent.
  - Tests: v7's own script from the tag against v8's on one fixture host, byte-identical bare and `--all` once v7's ledger lines are dropped; regenerated goldens; queue, migrate and fanout tests; the interface contract and architecture notes reconciled; manual read-only checks on `~/projects/niko-hub` and `~/projects/shop-bltr` recorded in the close commit.
- I will not touch:
  - The engine: `run.js`, worktrees, sidecars, fanout's lane mechanics beyond the one `after` clause, the queue file's location and verbs other than `add`; no `origin` field, no ledger, no doctor change.
  - v7's render and derivation code: no line is edited, added or reordered outside the named input functions and the four deleted ledger lines; a v7 line that reads oddly on v8 (the prototype-open paste names `spec-paths`) is carried, not fixed, and named below.
  - The plugin version (ADR-0039 D4: bumped at release).
- Architecture or behaviour change: yes — the queue model gains `type` and `after`, brief items complete with their brief, the roadmap's doneness rule is v7's, the screen drops every v8 render element and `--json` takes v7's shape, and ADR-0039 D6 is superseded (no ledger-derived status lines). JJ said yes on 2026-11-05 in the main session (AskUserQuestion); the amendment of the same day only removes v8 render elements and reads existing state, and is locked under that yes.
